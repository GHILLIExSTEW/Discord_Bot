PLAYMAKER PICKS TRACKER
PUBLIC GUIDE

WHAT THE TRACKER DOES

The Playmaker Picks tracker records eligible betting posts, tracks their results, and keeps a live summary of unit performance.

Only members with an approved Playmaker role are included in tracking and leaderboards.

HOW TO POST A BET

Post one bet per message and include the unit amount at the end.

Recommended format:

Team or player - bet details - 2U

Examples:

Yankees ML - 2U
Lakers -3.5 - 1U
Chiefs moneyline - 0.5U
Celtics spread - 1.5U

UNIT RULES

- Always include U after the unit amount.
- Whole numbers and decimals are supported.
- These formats are accepted: 1U, 2U, 0.5U, 1.5U, and 2 U.
- Uppercase and lowercase U are accepted.
- Use one bet per post.
- The tracker records the first unit amount it finds in a post.
- A number without U will not be counted.

HOW TO RECORD A RESULT

React to the original bet message:

✅ means the bet won.
❌ means the bet lost.

The tracker checks that the result reaction comes from an eligible Playmaker and then records the result.

If a result was added by mistake, remove the reaction. The matching result will be removed from the tracker.

Do not add both ✅ and ❌ to the same bet. If both are added, the tracker keeps the most recent result for that user and bet.

PENDING BETS

A new bet remains pending until an eligible Playmaker adds a ✅ or ❌ reaction.

Pending bets are displayed in the tracker summary. Old historical posts are not added back to the pending list during a history scan.

WHAT THE TRACKER SHOWS

The tracker message includes:

- Daily results
- 7-day performance
- Month-to-date performance
- Year-to-date performance
- All-time performance
- Pending bets
- Top Playmakers
- Full Playmaker breakdown
- Wins and losses
- Win/loss record
- Net units
- Win rate

The Playmaker breakdown only includes members who currently have an approved Playmaker role.

WHEN IT UPDATES

The tracker message updates when the bot starts and then refreshes every four hours.

The same tracker message is edited each time so the channel does not fill up with duplicates.

IMPORTANT REMINDERS

- Post bets in the monitored betting channel.
- Include the unit amount in every bet post.
- Use one bet per message.
- Add a result reaction when the bet is settled.
- Remove an incorrect reaction instead of adding a conflicting result.
- Only approved Playmakers are included in the tracker.
